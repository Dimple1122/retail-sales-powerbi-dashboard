📊 Retail Sales Performance Dashboard — Power BI
This project analyzes a retail sales dataset across three years to derive business insights on revenue trends, category performance, product contribution, and profit margins using Power BI.

🛠 Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query (Data Cleaning)
- DAX Measures
- Star Schema Data Modeling

📌 KPIs Designed
- Total Revenue, Profit, Profit Margin
- Quantity Sold, Total Customers
- Quarterly & Yearly Trends
- Category & Product Performance

🔍 Key Insights
- Overall revenue shows a year-on-year decline from 2022 to 2024
- Groceries category contributes the highest revenue among all categories and it remains the highest revenue-generating category across years
- Top 10 products contribute ~60% of total revenue (Pareto effect)
- Furniture category yields the highest profit margin (36.3%) despite lower sales volume
- Groceries and Electronics categories were the primary contributors to the revenue drop, while Furniture category showed growth but couldn’t offset the decline
